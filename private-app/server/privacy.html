Data and privacy information · Updated 25 October 2026

What is stored

Google sign-in provides your Google account identifier, verified email address, name and profile image. The service stores authentication sessions, including technical details such as browser information and IP address, and the work you choose to save: tasks, deadlines, categories, income figures, notes, team content and membership records.

Who can see your work

The designated owner can access their personal workspace. Invited members can access only their authorized team spaces. Viewer access is read-only; Editors can update shared content. Membership does not grant access to the owner's personal tasks, income or private notes. Invitation links are tied to a Google email, expire after 24 hours and are single-use.

Services used

Render hosts the application server and Supabase hosts its PostgreSQL database. Google provides authentication. Data passes through the application server over HTTPS; the database connection uses verified TLS. Credentials used by the server stay outside the public source repository. Storage is not end-to-end encrypted: authorized service and database administrators may access stored data.

Optional Calendar connection

Calendar access requires a separate Google consent. Permission is limited to calendars created by this app. The integration sends task deadlines to a dedicated calendar; event titles are generic by default, with task titles available as an opt-in setting. Income amounts and private notes are excluded. Apple Calendar can display this calendar after you add the Google account on your iPhone. Stopping sync leaves previously created events in the calendar.

Sessions, exports and controls

The app uses an essential HttpOnly session cookie and locks after 30 minutes without activity. The cloud version does not persist workspace records in localStorage. You can export a backup, edit or delete work, revoke your sessions and manage team access. Downloaded backups and team snapshots are readable files outside later access revocation; store and share them carefully. Unsynced drafts stay in memory and may be lost if you close the page.

Records remain until deleted or removed through workspace administration, subject to provider backup retention and operational logs. Contact the owner for removal requests that are not available in the interface. No advertising or third-party analytics tools are included in the application.
